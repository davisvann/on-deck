# Recording the demo (under 3 minutes)

Run in Terminal from this folder: `npm run dev`, then in a second window `npm run simulate`. The simulator opens in demo mode. Or use the real glasses with demo mode switched on in the phone screen.

For live Claude drafting and real voice, paste a Claude key (and a Deepgram key for voice) on the phone screen with demo mode still on. Nothing is ever sent in demo mode.

| Beat | Do this | The lens shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open On Deck. On a fresh install, tap "try the demo" | "Claude is reading your day..." (or "Loading sample cards..."), then card 1 of 5 |
| 2 | Flight card: tap | "Sending in 3...", then "Reply sent to Priya" |
| 3 | Sam card: swipe down, say "Tell Sam I'll be ten minutes late", tap. Then tap to send | Your words, "Claude is revising...", the new draft, then "Reply sent to Sam". Without keys: hold for the menu and pick "I'll be 10 minutes late" |
| 4 | Sponsorship pitch: swipe up | Skipped, the next card |
| 5 | Calendar and prep cards: tap each | Then "All clear. Head up." with the day summary |

Say once on camera: "Claude does the ranking, the drafting and the revising. Gmail and Calendar are the sources."
